Uno's First · mobile-first review

The pass feels warm, polished, and on-theme.

The racing language, paper texture, and credential-like card make the contribution feel ceremonial. The main opportunity is to make the success moment faster to scan—and to give it one clear next move.

Recommendation: keep this direction. Tighten the top-of-screen success signal, increase a few small-text contrasts, and let the pass become the undisputed hero.
1

Strong hierarchy inside the pass

The donor name is the largest element, and the Fuel / Pass No. / Season strip is quick to understand. It reads like a keepsake rather than a generic receipt.

2

Too much competing “thank you” copy

The introduction above, the note below, and the CTA all have similar visual weight. On a short viewport, they make the card feel less immediate.

Change: shorten the top copy to one line and remove or reduce the lower thank-you note. Let the pass arrive closer to the header.

3

Accessibility needs one contrast pass

The pale gray body text, pale blue certificate label, date, and signature are attractive but will be difficult for some people on a phone, especially outdoors.

Change: use a darker gray for all small text; reserve low-opacity blue only for decoration. Keep body text at 14–16 px where possible.

4

The action label is slightly ambiguous

“Back to the race” is charming, but it does not tell a first-time donor whether it closes this pass, returns to the event site, or begins another contribution.

Change: label it “Back to Uno’s birthday” or “Return to the race,” and make the close icon at least a 44 × 44 px target.

5

Use the empty bottom space intentionally

At this height, the footer has generous breathing room, which is lovely. If the screen can scroll, consider a secondary “Save your pass” or “Share with the crew” action after the primary return button—only if those actions work end-to-end. Otherwise, leave the calm space; it supports the premium keepsake feeling.

Quick mobile QA
  • Test with a 320 px-wide device and a long donor name (25+ characters).
  • Verify no text becomes smaller than the rest of the app’s readable body copy.
  • Confirm focus, Escape, and screen-reader behavior for the drawer and close control.
  • Make the pass downloadable/shareable only if it is a real post-payment confirmation.
